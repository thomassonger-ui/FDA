## The 3 required components (uploaded together as one submission)

1. **Final artifact** — original work demonstrating radiology knowledge. Pick one of the acceptable artifact types below. Submit as a file (PDF, PNG, JPG, PPTX, MP4 under 200 MB) or paste an unlisted YouTube/Vimeo link in the online text field.
2. **Metacognition statement** (online text, ≥ 200 words) — why this week, why this format, what the student learned about their own learning process while building it, and how they'll use the artifact in practice. Written in their own voice.
3. **Sources cited** — Bird & Robinson textbook, at least one course lesson or Pressbook activity, and any other source drawn on. AI disclosure if applicable.

---

## Acceptable artifact types

- Photos of instrument placement on a coworker
- Dentist-approved anonymized images
- Original infographic
- Short explainer video
- Patient-facing handbook

These are categories of *content*. The file/format options listed in component 1 (PDF, PNG, JPG, PPTX, MP4, YouTube/Vimeo link) apply on top — any of the artifact types above may be submitted in any acceptable file format.

---

## Artifact requirements (all four must apply)

- Must be the student's original work
- Must demonstrate radiology knowledge
- Dentist verification may be required (for clinical content)
- No identifiable patient information

These are non-negotiable. Submissions failing any of the four are returned via "Needs Revision" with the failing item cited in instructor feedback.

## Grading

Per [`REFERENCE-capstone-rubric.md`](./REFERENCE-capstone-rubric.md): graded Complete / Needs Revision. Maximum 50 points; pass = 38 (76%). All 5 rubric criteria must score Proficient (≥ 2) for Complete.

This Capstone (cmid 238) is one of the two items that count toward the course total (Final Exam 50 pts + Capstone 50 pts = 100). It is also one of the three gating items on `Download Your Certificate` (cmid 302) — must be graded (≥ 0%) for the cert restriction to clear.
