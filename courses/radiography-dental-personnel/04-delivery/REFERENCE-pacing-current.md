# REFERENCE — Pacing Calendar (current 14-hour architecture)

Target pace for each week of the 6-week program. **This file replaces the legacy `REFERENCE-6-week-pacing.md` (which described the 56-hour online + 8-hour clinical-day version).**

Pace targets reflect the canonical 14-hour breakdown: **8 theory + 6 lab + 0 externship.** Lab hours include simulation-based practice plus the 6-hour Capstone Project (split 2.5 hr Week 5 + 3.5 hr Week 6).

## Per-week deliverables

### Week 1 — RHS101 (~2 hr)
- Read Bird & Robinson Ch 38 (Foundations)
- 3 external-practice lessons (Dose-Response Curve, Radiation Risks & ALARA, Collimation)
- Knowledge Check Week 1 (formative; ≥75% to advance)
- Self-Reflection Week 1 (200 words; required for Final Exam unlock)

### Week 2 — RHS102 (~3 hr)
- Read Bird & Robinson Ch 39 (Digital Imaging, Film & Processing)
- 3 external-practice lessons (Image Mounts, Processing Steps, Processing Errors)
- KC Week 2 + Self-Reflection Week 2

### Week 3 — RHS103 (~3 hr)
- Read Bird & Robinson Ch 40 (Legal, QA, Infection Prevention)
- 3 external-practice lessons (Infection Control Terminology, Hand Hygiene, Patient Education FAQ)
- KC Week 3 + Self-Reflection Week 3

### Week 4 — RHS104 (~4 hr)
- Read Bird & Robinson Ch 41 (Intraoral Imaging)
- 3 external-practice lessons (XCP Assembly, Bisecting Angulation, Cone-Cut Recognition)
- KC Week 4 + Self-Reflection Week 4

### Week 5 — RHS105 (~4.5 hr including Capstone Phase 1)
- Read Bird & Robinson Ch 42 (Extraoral) + Ch 41 Lesson 41.2 Error Catalog
- 3 external-practice lessons (Panoramic Anatomy, Panoramic Positioning Errors, Skull Imaging)
- KC Week 5 + Self-Reflection Week 5
- **Capstone Project Phase 1 (2.5 hr):** read the rubric, choose week + artifact format, plan, build first draft, submit plan + draft

### Week 6 — Final Competency (~3.5 hr Capstone + assessment time)
- 3 simulation-prep lessons (Paralleling PA Placement, Bitewing Beam Alignment, Maxillary Occlusal Projection)
- Final Exam (50 pts, 30 min, 2 attempts)
- Clinical Verification Form (signed by supervising Florida-licensed dentist)
- **Capstone Project Phase 2 (3.5 hr):** refine artifact, write 200-word metacognition statement, cite sources, submit
- Capstone Self-Reflection (200 words)
- Certificate page unlocks once Verification Form + Capstone Reflection are complete

## Cohort cadence assumptions
- **Cohort start:** Monday of Week 1
- **Weekly check-ins:** optional Tuesday 7 PM live with Debbie Sanders (Instructor of Record)
- **Submission cadence:** weekly deliverables due Sunday 11:59 PM Eastern
- **Final week:** entire Week 6 due by end of cohort window
- **Records retention:** all student submissions retained for 4 years per FAC 64B5-9.011

## Pacing flexibility
The course is structured across 6 weeks but allows flexibility within each cohort window. Students may move faster than the suggested pacing provided they complete each week's prerequisites in sequence (Chapter Book → 3 Lessons → KC + Self-Reflection) before unlocking the Final Exam in Week 6. Students who need adjusted pacing for medical, caregiving, or scheduling needs may request accommodations through the Student Support process.
