# REFERENCE — Week Specs (RHS101–RHS105 + Week 6)

Build-ready specifications for each of the six weeks. **This file replaces the legacy `REFERENCE-module-specs.md` (M1–M12), which is archived.**

All weekly Knowledge Checks: 5 scenario MCQs, 10-min timer, 2 attempts (higher score recorded), ≥75% to pass, full feedback after each attempt, shuffled question + answer order. KCs are formative — required for Final Exam unlock but weight = 0 in gradebook.

---

## Week 1 — RHS101 · Foundations of Radiography, Equipment, and Radiation Safety
- **Hours:** 2 (1 theory + 1 lab) · **FDOE alignment:** Standard 17.01, 17.02, 17.03
- **Required reading:** Bird & Robinson Chapter 38 (cmid 199)
- **Topics:** (1) Radiation basics — what X-rays are, ionizing radiation, dose units (mSv, Gy); (2) ALARA principles — time/distance/shielding, justification, optimization; (3) Patient and operator safety — lead aprons, thyroid collars, dosimeter use, occupational limits, pregnancy; (4) Equipment overview — tubehead anatomy, control panel, exposure factors (kVp, mA, time)
- **External-practice lessons:** Dose-Response Curve (Pressbook ch 3.1) · Radiation Risks & ALARA (Pressbook ch 3.2) · Collimation (Pressbook ch 4.1)
- **Embedded activity:** GitHub-hosted iframe — *X-Ray Machine Component Parts* drag-the-labels

## Week 2 — RHS102 · Dental Imaging, Dental Film, and Processing Radiographs
- **Hours:** 3 (2 theory + 1 lab) · **FDOE alignment:** Standard 17.04, 17.05
- **Required reading:** Bird & Robinson Chapter 39 (cmid 200)
- **Topics:** (1) Digital sensors (CCD, CMOS) and PSP plates; (2) Traditional intraoral film — D, E, F-speed; (3) Processing chemistry — developer, rinse, fixer, wash, dry; (4) Image mounting and quality control
- **External-practice lessons:** Maxillary & Mandibular Image Mounts (Pressbook ch 11.2) · Manual Film Processing Steps (Pressbook ch 10.2) · Film Processing Errors (Pressbook ch 10.3)

## Week 3 — RHS103 · Legal Issues, Quality Assurance, and Infection Prevention
- **Hours:** 3 (2 theory + 1 lab) · **FDOE alignment:** Standard 17.06, 17.07
- **Required reading:** Bird & Robinson Chapter 40 (cmid 202)
- **Topics:** (1) Florida regulation — FAC 64B5-9.011 supervision, scope of practice, §466.024(6) F.S. statutory authority; (2) Infection control and standard precautions; (3) Quality assurance — equipment QA, processing checks, retake logs; (4) Patient relations and informed consent
- **External-practice lessons:** Infection Control Terminology (Pressbook ch 16.1) · Hand Hygiene (Pressbook ch 16.1) · Patient Education FAQ (Pressbook ch 14.1)

## Week 4 — RHS104 · Intraoral Imaging
- **Hours:** 4 (2 theory + 2 lab) · **FDOE alignment:** Standard 17.08
- **Required reading:** Bird & Robinson Chapter 41 (cmid 203)
- **Topics:** (1) Periapical (PA) technique — paralleling vs. bisecting angle; (2) Bitewing technique — horizontal beam alignment, vertical angulation; (3) Receptor placement, holders (XCP/Rinn), beam alignment devices; (4) Common technique errors — cone cuts, foreshortening, elongation, overlap, missed apices
- **External-practice lessons:** XCP Holder Assembly (Pressbook ch 19.1) · Bisecting Angulation Practice (Pressbook ch 20.1) · Cone-Cut Recognition (Pressbook ch 22.2)

## Week 5 — RHS105 · Extraoral Imaging
- **Hours:** 2 (1 theory + 1 lab) · **FDOE alignment:** Standard 17.09
- **Required reading:** Bird & Robinson Chapter 42 (cmid 201) + Chapter 41 Lesson 41.2 Error Catalog (cmid 204)
- **Topics:** (1) Panoramic radiography — technique, focal trough, common errors; (2) Cephalometric radiography — orthodontic growth analysis; (3) Cone Beam CT (CBCT) — 3D imaging fundamentals; (4) Skull views (Waters, Towne, lateral) for sinus / TMJ / trauma
- **External-practice lessons:** Panoramic X-Ray Unit Anatomy (Pressbook ch 25.1) · Panoramic Patient Positioning Errors (Pressbook ch 25.2) · Skull Imaging Projections (Pressbook ch 26.2)
- **Capstone Project Phase 1:** Plan & Draft (2.5 lab hours) — student picks one week of content + one of three artifact formats

## Week 6 — Final Competency & Evaluation
- **Hours:** not counted in clock hours (assessment week)
- **Activities:** Simulation prep (3 lessons — PA / BW / Occlusal) → Final Exam (25 MCQs, 30 min, 2 attempts, ≥75% to pass) → Clinical Verification Form (signed by supervising Florida-licensed dentist) → Capstone Project Phase 2 (Final Submission, 3.5 lab hours, 50 points) → Capstone Reflection (200-word individual) → Certificate
- **Total Capstone time across Weeks 5–6:** 6 lab hours (2.5 + 3.5)

## Knowledge Check item-bank approach
Each weekly KC = 5 scenario-based items drawn week-specific. No shared bank across weeks. Items are recall-application hybrids, not pure recall.

## Final Exam item composition
25 scenario items, 2 points each (50 marks total), drawn proportionally from the 5 RHS courses. Locked review options to protect the question bank for future cohorts.
