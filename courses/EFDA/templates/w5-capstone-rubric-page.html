Capstone Rubric & Self-Check Guide

Read this before you start your Plan & Draft. Every point on your final Capstone is scored against the six criteria below.

Why this matters

The Capstone is your evidence that you can perform an EFDA procedure safely, correctly, and to the standard the Florida Board of Dentistry expects. The rubric below is exactly how Ashley and Debbie score your Week-6 submission — no surprises. Use the self-check at the bottom to pressure-test your work before you hit Submit.

50 points total

Grading rubric

Criterion Pts Strong · Adequate · Needs Revision
1. Procedure accuracy
Steps performed in correct order, with correct technique		 10 Strong (9-10): Every step correct and in textbook sequence. Adequate (6-8): One step out of order or a minor technique gap. Needs Revision (0-5): Two or more steps missing or incorrect.
2. Safety / Infection control
PPE, barriers, sterilization sequencing, anti-PHI photo practice		 10 Strong (9-10): Full PPE, barrier setup, and zero PHI exposure. Adequate (6-8): One minor lapse you self-noted. Needs Revision (0-5): Missing PPE step, broken barrier, or any PHI visible in photos.
3. Documentation quality
Activity log entries, supervising dentist initials/dates, completeness		 8 Strong (7-8): Every field complete, dentist signature + date present, legible. Adequate (5-6): One field missing or unclear. Needs Revision (0-4): Multiple gaps or missing dentist attestation.
4. Photo evidence completeness
All required shots captured, well-framed, demonstrably the procedure stated		 8 Strong (7-8): All required photos present, sharp, framed tightly on procedure area. Adequate (5-6): One photo missing or out of focus. Needs Revision (0-4): Two or more photos missing or unusable.
5. Professional judgment
When to act, when to ask the dentist, when to stop — demonstrated decisions		 7 Strong (6-7): Reflection clearly shows decisions made and rationale. Adequate (4-5): Decisions described but rationale thin. Needs Revision (0-3): No reflection on judgment calls.
6. Alignment with EFDA competencies
Maps clearly to EFDA101–113 scope; nothing outside scope claimed		 7 Strong (6-7): Procedure clearly labeled with EFDA code; everything performed is within scope. Adequate (4-5): Code labeled but one minor scope ambiguity. Needs Revision (0-3): Out-of-scope task performed or no EFDA code identified.
Total 50 Passing for certificate: ≥ 38 of 50 (75%)

Self-check before you submit

Run this checklist against your own work. Answer each one honestly. If any answer is “no,” revise before you hit Submit.

  1. Procedure accuracy: Did I perform every step in textbook order, and can I name each step on the spot?
  2. Safety: Is there zero patient identifying information in any of my photos? Can I show full PPE in the photos that include me?
  3. Documentation: Are all dentist initials and dates present and legible on the activity log?
  4. Photos: Do my photos clearly show the procedure I claim, framed tight enough that anyone scoring can verify what they’re looking at?
  5. Judgment: Did I write a short reflection on at least one decision I made (when to call the dentist, what I would do differently)?
  6. Scope: Is my procedure labeled with its EFDA code (EFDA101–113), and is everything I performed within that scope?
