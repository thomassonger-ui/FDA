Capstone Project — Plan & Draft

A one-page proposal of what you’ll demonstrate for your Week-6 Capstone. Submit before the week ends so your instructor can flag any gaps before you spend serious time on the final.

Why this matters

Walking into the Week-6 Capstone with a vetted plan is how you protect your 50 points. The instructor reviews your Plan & Draft within 2 business days and flags anything that would cost you on the rubric — out-of-scope tasks, PHI exposure risk in photos, missing dentist attestation, or weak procedure choice. Fix it once here, not after grading.

What your Plan & Draft must include

  1. EFDA procedure — name the one procedure you will demonstrate. List its EFDA code (EFDA101–113) and the Robinson 14e chapter that anchors it.
  2. Format — pick ONE: infographic · PowerPoint deck (8–12 slides) · hand-drawn or digital illustration · photo essay (4–6 photos with captions).
  3. Photo / evidence plan — list each photo or artifact you intend to capture (pre-op, mid-procedure, post-op, etc.). Note for each: typodont or live patient.
  4. Anti-PHI plan — how will you guarantee zero patient identifiers in every submitted image? Reference the HIPAA Acknowledgment if you need to refresh.
  5. Dentist alignment — one sentence confirming your supervising Florida-licensed dentist knows you’re documenting this procedure and will be available to sign your activity log.
  6. Self-check against the rubric — for each of the 6 rubric criteria, write one sentence on how your plan addresses it.

Submission specs

  • 1 file · PDF or DOCX preferred · up to 10 MB
  • 1 page (250–400 words is the right depth)
  • Plain prose — no need to format heavily. Bullet points fine.
  • Reviewed within 2 business days. Feedback returned via Moodle comment.
Formative — not graded

This Plan & Draft is ungraded. It carries no points toward your course total. Its only purpose is to surface fixable problems before you sink real time into the 50-point Week-6 Capstone. Treat it seriously and the feedback will save you points on the final.
